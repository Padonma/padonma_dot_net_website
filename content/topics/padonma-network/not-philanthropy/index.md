Sieze the means of production? Sure, got that with just a backstrap
loom and a supply of fiber. What we're doing here is "Seizing the
means of distribution." Although the strategy of open source software
(OSS) is not exactly seizing control but distributing it, especially
because the Padonma Network has been designed to be extremely simply
yet capable of scaling globally, internet-scale via Google's search
engine.
