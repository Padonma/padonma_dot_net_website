Globally all lotus textile mills are located in Southeast Asia. Lotus
textile production was invented in the early 1900s in Burma. In recent
years manufacturing has spread -- in order -- to Cambodia, Vietnam,
India, and most recently Bangladesh.

Some textile terminology:
- Fiber: the naturally occurring loose stringy bits that nature gives us.
- Yarn: fibers twisted, spun, or -- an in the case of lotus -- rolled into a continuous strand.
- Fabric: yarns assembled into a flat structure. For lotus that is tradtionally implies handlooming.
- Mill: a mill produces yarn and/or fabric.

This page lists suppliers of both lotus yarn and lotus fabric.
