Phan Thị Thuận is the founder and director of **Mỹ Đức Silk and
Sericulture Co., Ltd.**, whose consumer-facing silk brand is **SILK4WORLD**.
The company is based in Phùng Xá, Mỹ Đức, near Hanoi, and produces silk and
lotus-silk textiles.
