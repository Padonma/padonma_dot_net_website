There is some wildly sophisticated styling to be found in the historical
and still living cultures within Burma. Some of it is palaciously
luxurious but that is not a goal of Atelier Padonma. And the ethnic
tribal fashion is even wilder and would make for beautiful clothing
but is simply too folkloric for the Atelier. But a coutry which counts
135 indiginous ethinic identities is fertile pickings for inspiration.

## "Inspired but not folkloric"

Jan-Jan Van Essche: [~"Build a pattern language based in the cultural roots but then without making it folkloric"](https://youtu.be/RLqTV8OSCUc?t=374)
