The Atelier serves primarily as a connector of small lotus fiber
producers with the atelier's of the global fashion capitals. Curiously
it is the Big Four (Paris, Milan, London, and New York) that need more
educating than the newer Asian markets such as Tokyo, Seoul, Shanghai,
Hong Kong which seemingly have a slightly more elevated awareness of
how luxurious and exclusive lotus fiber is.
!

So, yes, Atelier Padonma does indeed produce lotus fiber apparel but
with the intent of getting the fabric touched by artisans at true
ateliers. After photoshoots showing off the few garments that Atelier
Padonma actually produces, the products end up in the hands of
customers who want the garments but realize that what the reason they
are paying haute couture prices is that they are actually funding the
bootstrapping of the Padonma Network.


Or at least that is the plan; if things work out well perhaps Atelier
Padonma becomes an real brand sourcing lotus fabric from suppliers at
some scale to crank out garments. Notice that Atelier Padonma focuses
on resort attire based on design that are historically Burmese. In this
way we stay out of sectors which true fashion houses service while still
having a focused theme to the atelier's product line.


## Business goal: connector and bootstrapper

It may well be that Atelier Padonma becomes a brand known for putting
its name on excellent lotus fiber apparel sourced from various
artisans, with the brand name serving as a proxy for quality, while
simultaneously anyone is free to join the Padonma Network without any
quality controls and no association or profit sharing with Atelier
Padonma.

Further, there is nothing about the technology developed for the
Padonma Network that is specific to lotus fiber. Any textile artisan
who can handweave a QR code (which the Padonma Network has endevoured
to make as easy as possible to adopt) can get on the network and
asvertise their goods no matter what fiber they are made of. Atelier
Padonma started on the lotus fiber part because its intense manual
labor causing natural scarcity and novelty make it an excellent
candidate for selling into the haute couture world.
