## Contemporary in Japan

Notice that the Prime Minister's wife, Akie Abe, is wearing a Burmese
outfit including a longyi with tradtional Burmese weaving Acheik wavy
patterning which can only be made by hand, usually in silk.



## Kachin

1913: “the Kachin woman is second to none in Burma when is comes to
artistic weaving and embroidery.” (Rev. O. Hanson, author of The
Kachins: Their Customs and Traditions 1913: 48)
